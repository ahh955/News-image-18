A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of **Wednesday, 18 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image. The date is in the masthead in large type, and five stories from five regions share the page:

1. **Europe — Kerch Bridge collapses.** The bridge to Crimea partially collapses under a second day of Ukrainian strikes; one arch falls into the strait.
2. **Middle East — US strikes Qeshm.** US fighter jets strike an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island; Iran fires drones at Bahrain and Kuwait in retaliation.
3. **United States — measles-free status lost.** The US loses its measles-free status after 26 years, with over 5,400 cases and 10 deaths in 2026.
4. **Alaska, USA — Peltola flips the Senate seat.** Democrat Mary Peltola wins Alaska's Senate seat, giving Democrats 56 seats in the US Senate.
5. **Africa — Ebola doubles in Congo.** The Ebola outbreak in DR Congo more than doubles, with over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths.

The panel artwork is original generated imagery (no copied photos); all text is drawn in clean vector type. The program (`main.ts`, Node stdlib only, no network) copies `entry.png` to the output directory, so repeated runs produce byte-identical output.
